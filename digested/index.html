From directed polymers to the heat flow

Tune the disorder strength to the critical window as the length of a directed polymer in two spatial dimensions grows. The diffusively rescaled field of partition functions converges in law to the stochastic heat flow, a process of random measures. Caravenna, Sun and Zygouras (2023)

From the heat flow to KPZ

Fix a bounded continuous initial height and a positive time. Convolve the corresponding heat flow solution with the heat kernel, take the logarithm and subtract the expected logarithm for flat initial data. As the smoothing scale tends to zero, the centered logarithm converges in probability to the critical 2D KPZ field in every local Sobolev space of negative order. Centered log partition functions of critical Gaussian directed polymers converge in law to the same field. Berger et al., Theorem 1.2 and Corollary 6.2.
